# MyCrew Support & FAQ

**Contact:** [mohamed-aero@hotmail.com](mailto:mohamed-aero@hotmail.com) — include the app name, your iOS version and a short description of the problem.

## MyCrew Roster

**Who can use it?** Air Algérie flight crew with an eCrew account. You sign in with your eCrew user ID and password; they are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

**How does it get my roster?** From eCrew, each time you open the app or pull to refresh. The last roster stays available offline.

**My roster changed — how do I know?** Changed duties are flagged so you can review and acknowledge them.

**How do I share my roster with my family?** In MyCrew Roster open Hub › Settings › Partner Sharing and send the invitation, or show the QR code. Your partner opens it with MyCrew Partner. You can stop sharing at any time.

**How do I delete my data?** Sign out to remove your credentials and cached roster from the device. For data stored on our server (chat, push token, marketplace listings, diagnostics), email us with your crew ID.

## MyCrew Partner

**Do I need an account?** No. Your crew member shares their roster with you through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone.

**The roster is not updating.** Open the app with an internet connection; if it still does not update, ask your crew member to tap Sync Now in MyCrew Roster's Partner Sharing screen.

**Can I try it without being paired?** Yes — tap "Try Demo" on the welcome screen to explore with sample data.
